## Market Position Summary

AccuLynx is the established market leader in roofing-specific business management software. They have:
- First-mover advantage in the space
- Strong brand recognition
- Extensive integration ecosystem
- Proven track record with large contractors
- Premium positioning in pricing

This creates opportunities for a competitor to:
- Target underserved segments (smaller contractors, price-sensitive buyers)
- Offer more modern UX/UI
- Provide better value at lower price points
- Include emerging features (AI, automation)
- Offer more flexible pricing models
