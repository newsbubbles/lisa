---

## What is Insurance Restoration?

Insurance restoration is a specialized segment of the roofing industry where contractors work on properties damaged by storms, hail, wind, or other insured events. This workflow involves:

1. **Storm Chasing** - Identifying and canvassing storm-damaged areas
2. **Lead Generation** - Door-to-door canvassing in affected neighborhoods
3. **Inspection** - Documenting damage for insurance claims
4. **Claims Filing** - Working with homeowners to file insurance claims
5. **Adjuster Coordination** - Meeting with insurance adjusters
6. **Scope Agreement** - Agreeing on repair scope and pricing
7. **Supplement Filing** - Requesting additional coverage for missed items
8. **Production** - Completing the repair work
9. **Payment Collection** - Collecting from insurance and homeowner

## Xactimate Integration Details

### What is Xactimate?
- Industry-standard estimating software used by insurance companies
- Provides standardized pricing database for repairs
- Creates estimates that insurance companies accept
- Used by adjusters, contractors, and restoration companies

### Integration Requirements
- Import Xactimate estimates into roofing software
- Export scopes to Xactimate format
- Sync pricing with Xactimate database
- Match line items between systems
- Track variances between contractor estimate and insurance estimate

### Xactimate Pricing Categories for Roofing
- Tear-off (per square)
- Underlayment (per square)
- Shingles (per square, by type)
- Flashing (linear feet)
- Ridge cap (linear feet)
- Drip edge (linear feet)
- Vents (each)
- Pipe boots (each)
- Labor rates (varies by region)
- Overhead & Profit (typically 20%)

## Storm Chasing Workflow (SPOTIO Model)

### Pre-Storm Preparation
1. Monitor weather forecasts
2. Identify potential storm paths
3. Pre-position canvassing teams
4. Prepare marketing materials

### Immediate Response (0-48 hours)
1. Confirm storm damage in area
2. Deploy canvassing teams
3. Begin door-to-door outreach
4. Document damage on properties
5. Schedule inspections

### Canvassing Workflow
1. **Territory Assignment** - Assign streets/neighborhoods to reps
2. **Door Knock** - Initial contact with homeowner
3. **Quick Inspection** - Visual assessment of damage
4. **Lead Capture** - Collect contact information
5. **Appointment Setting** - Schedule detailed inspection
6. **Door Status Update** - Mark result in system
7. **Route Continuation** - Move to next property

### Key Metrics to Track
- Doors knocked per hour
- Appointments set per day
- Conversion rate (doors to appointments)
- Conversion rate (appointments to contracts)
- Average job value
- Close rate by rep
- Territory coverage percentage
