# Lisa - Executive Summary

**Project:** Lisa - AccuLynx Competitor  
**Date:** 2026-03-07  
**Status:** Planning Complete

---

## Project Overview

Lisa is a modern, cloud-based roofing business management platform designed to compete with AccuLynx, the current market leader. The product will differentiate through:

1. **Modern UX/UI** - Clean, intuitive design vs. dated competitors
2. **AI-Powered Features** - Lead scoring, instant estimates, writing assistance
3. **Transparent Pricing** - Published pricing 30-50% below AccuLynx
4. **Mobile-First** - Superior mobile experience
5. **Flexible Scaling** - From solo contractors to enterprise

---

## Market Opportunity

### Target Market
- **Primary:** Small to mid-size roofing contractors (1-50 employees)
- **Market Size:** 50,000+ roofing contractors in the US
- **Pain Points:** High software costs, complex tools, poor mobile experience

### Competitive Landscape
| Competitor | Strength | Weakness | Our Opportunity |
|------------|----------|----------|------------------|
| AccuLynx | Market leader, comprehensive | Expensive, dated UX | Better value, modern UX |
| JobNimbus | AI features, good UX | Complex pricing | Simpler, transparent |
| Roofr | Affordable, easy | Limited features | More comprehensive |
| RoofSnap | Good measurements | Narrow focus | Full platform |

---

## Product Strategy

### Phase 1: MVP (Months 1-4)
**Goal:** Launch functional product with core features

- Core CRM (contacts, leads, pipeline)
- Estimating with EagleView integration
- Proposal generation with e-signature
- Job management (kanban board)
- QuickBooks integration
- Basic mobile app
- Payment processing

### Phase 2: Growth (Months 5-8)
**Goal:** Feature parity with competitors

- HOVER integration
- Supplier integrations (Beacon, ABC)
- SMS messaging
- Automated workflows
- Advanced mobile features
- Financing integration

### Phase 3: Differentiation (Months 9-12)
**Goal:** Unique value proposition

- Insurance restoration module
- AI-powered features
- Advanced analytics
- Customer portal
- API marketplace
